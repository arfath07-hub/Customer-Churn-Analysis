# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges faced by telecom companies. Retaining existing customers is more cost-effective than acquiring new ones, making churn analysis a critical business problem.

This project performs an end-to-end customer churn analysis using **Python, SQL, Power BI, and Machine Learning** to identify the key factors influencing customer churn, predict customers who are likely to leave, and provide actionable business recommendations for improving customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze customer churn behavior.
* Clean and preprocess raw customer data.
* Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
* Identify the major factors affecting customer churn.
* Build a Machine Learning model to predict customer churn.
* Create an interactive Power BI dashboard.
* Generate business insights and recommendations for decision-making.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* Customers with **Month-to-Month contracts** have the highest churn rate.
* Customers using **Fiber Optic** internet services are more likely to churn than DSL users.
* Customers paying through **Electronic Check** show higher churn.
* Customers with **shorter tenure** are at greater risk of leaving.
* Higher **Monthly Charges** are associated with increased churn.
* Customers using **Online Security** and **Tech Support** services have better retention.

---

## 📌 Business Recommendations

* Encourage customers to switch from Month-to-Month to long-term contracts.
* Offer retention incentives for high-risk Fiber Optic customers.
* Improve customer onboarding during the first few months.
* Promote Online Security and Tech Support services.
* Provide personalized offers to customers with high monthly charges.
